Kage-9 Rising Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Kage-9 Rising is an offline Android game published by Shotgun Ninjas Productions, LLC. This policy covers Android package com.shotgunninjas.kage9, beginning with version 1.0.1.

Information handled by the game

The game does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information to us. It contains no advertising, in-app purchases, analytics SDKs or developer-operated tracking.

The application runs using included assets. It does not request Internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts or access to shared files.

Progress and settings

The game saves campaign progress, checkpoints, mission unlocks, performance records, difficulty, and audio and graphics preferences in local application storage. This lets you resume play and remember your choices. The information is not sent to us.

Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling removes your saved progress and preferences. The app is configured to exclude its data from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Sharing and third parties

The game does not sell or share gameplay or personal information with third parties. It does not use online advertising, social sign-in or cloud saves.

Google Play and Android may independently process information to distribute, update, secure or operate apps under their own policies and your device settings. This policy describes the Kage-9 Rising application, not those independent services.

Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and address support or privacy concerns. Our email provider processes the correspondence for delivery and storage. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

We retain correspondence only as reasonably needed to handle requests, maintain related support records or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting us. We cannot retrieve or delete game saves remotely because they remain on your device.

Children’s privacy

The game’s no-collection design applies to every player. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the game. A parent or guardian concerned about information separately sent to us may contact us below.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app’s privacy practices change and revise its effective date.
